# Luvani revamp B: illustrations

One illustrator, one hand. Flat fills from the palette only (lilac #B9A7FF is allowed here and nowhere
else), no gradients, no outlines except deliberate 3px ink or white strokes, corner radii 8, 10, 14 and
16, a shared confetti vocabulary (14 by 6 bars, dots, softened triangles, squiggles, rings), no words
and no faces anywhere. Paste the whole `<svg>` inline by id and set `width` and `height` to the
display size; every drawing scales cleanly because it is pure vector.

Grounds: place scenes and celebrations are transparent and sit on the colour card named in their
note. State marks sit on paper and carry ink lines so the white shapes read there. Mouth guides sit on
sun or paper. Postcards carry their own white border and sit on mint.
