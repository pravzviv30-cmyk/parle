- **Verdict: Quiet voice.**
- VAD thresholds with zero noise-only false starts (bed level and below): 0.5, 0.35, 0.25. Threshold needed: 0.5 (the defaults suffice).
- Speech RMS of the v3 plain and v2 normal clips at -20 dB: median -36.0 dBFS, against the meter floor of -40 dBFS (0.01). The meter would move.
- The plugin energy filter matters at the bottom: speech whose 100 ms RMS stays at or under 0.004 (about -48 dBFS) is never sent to Deepgram once the stream has been quiet for a second, whatever Deepgram could have made of it. The lead here spends that cooldown, as it is spent mid-session.
- Synthetic caveat: every whispered clip is ElevenLabs v3 with an audio tag, not a learner. Gain is a digital attenuation of studio audio, which is not the same as a person speaking softly into a phone (a real whisper also changes articulation, and phone AGC may lift it). Treat this as a screen; founder recordings in `artifacts/quiet-speech-2026-10-23/real/` are the next check.
- The commit replay uses an EOT probability of 1 (the fallback floor), so "Commits" says whether the turn ends at all with quiet speech, not when the model would hold it.
