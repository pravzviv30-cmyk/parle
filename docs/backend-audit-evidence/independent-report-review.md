## Stale credentials/readiness/targets

Draft correctly avoids repeating that retired credentials remain live. F80's 3October update and continuation record human retirement confirmation plus replacement auth checks; no new credential verification was run by this challenge reviewer. Optionally make explicit that old-key invalidation could not independently be replayed because historical objects were already pruned. Do not convert replacement-key authentication into proof all possible past keys are revoked.

Public-launch HOLD is well supported by reproduced availability/admission defects and missing operational acceptance. Leading-app architectural comparison remains qualitative, not private-topology certification. Proposed99.9%,99.5%,5s,3s and10/50/100 staging tiers are clearly labeled initial choices, not measured industry requirements or demonstrated Luvani performance. They should become owned/approved workload targets before release.
