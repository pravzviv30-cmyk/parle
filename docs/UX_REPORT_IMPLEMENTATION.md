5. Precise privacy/control, fair plans and free recovery.
6. B visual consistency, accessible driven journeys and measurement definitions.
7. Full source gate/export, independent review, iPhone/French review kit and landing reconciliation.

checks for them; do not treat their availability as completed verification.
